The "magical sky" release — a full visual overhaul of the star field and nebula:

• Star-forming cores: the densest gas now ignites into brilliant white-pink hearts (HDR)
• Realistic star colors: blackbody sequence, bright stars skew blue-white, faint ones warm
• Distant galaxies: tiny inclined spirals drift among the stars
• Star flares are now rare, long events instead of constant texture
• Faint milky-way band gives empty sky depth and resolves into star grain
• Slow palette moods: scenes occasionally drift warm-rose or deep blue
• Fixed star shimmer (grid aliasing); twinkle is now genuinely chaotic, like real scintillation
• Many more faint stars
