Dust, reflected starlight, and a camera that goes somewhere:

• Dust lanes carve dark channels through the clouds, and dense foreground dust now reaches real opacity, cutting silhouettes out of the star field behind it
• The gas is filamentary instead of billowing — masses gather into streamers and sheets rather than round lumps
• The brightest stars light the gas they sit in, with a blue-white reflection glow that only appears where there is cloud to scatter it
• The camera reads the sky ahead and steers toward richer regions instead of drifting blind
• A session now arrives: stars come up first, then the gas blooms in behind them while the camera closes distance
